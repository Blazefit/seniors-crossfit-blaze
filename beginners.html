🚫 Myths vs ✅ Reality

❌ MYTH: "I need to get in shape before starting CrossFit"

This is like saying you need to get clean before taking a shower. CrossFit IS how you get in shape. We've had members start who couldn't do a single push-up — now they're doing handstands.

✅ TRUTH: Every workout is scaled to YOUR level

Can't do a pull-up? We'll give you a band or ring row. Can't run? You'll bike or row. The whiteboard shows the workout — but YOUR version is designed just for you.

❌ MYTH: "CrossFit is dangerous and causes injuries"

Any physical activity can cause injury if done incorrectly. That's why EVERY class at CrossFit Blaze is coach-led. We teach proper form, correct movement patterns, and never push you beyond safe limits.

✅ TRUTH: Our coaches have been doing this since 2009

Owner-coached gym with over 15 years of experience. We've seen it all, trained every body type, and know how to keep you safe while getting results.

❌ MYTH: "Everyone will be staring at me"

Trust us — everyone is too focused on their own workout to notice anyone else. And the only looks you'll get are high-fives and encouragement. That's our culture.

Your First Class: What to Expect

10 MINUTES BEFORE

Arrive Early

Show up 10 minutes before class. A coach will greet you, show you around, and ask about any injuries or concerns. We'll explain how the class works.

0-15 MINUTES

Warm-Up

Every class starts with a group warm-up. Light movement, stretching, and mobility work to prepare your body. Nothing crazy — just getting loose.

15-30 MINUTES

Skill/Strength Work

We'll practice the movements in today's workout. The coach will demonstrate, then walk around helping everyone with form. Take your time here — this is where you learn.

30-50 MINUTES

The Workout (WOD)

Time to work! The "Workout of the Day" is usually 10-20 minutes of effort. Go at YOUR pace. The coach will suggest weights and modifications specifically for you.

50-60 MINUTES

Cool Down & High Fives

Stretching, breathing, and celebrating that you showed up. You did it! Members will likely introduce themselves and welcome you to the community.

What to Bring (Hint: Not Much)

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I come as a beginner?

Start with 2-3 classes per week. Your body needs time to adapt. As you get stronger and more comfortable, you can increase to 4-5 times per week. Listen to your body — soreness is normal, pain is not.

I'm over 50 — is CrossFit right for me?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated seniors program and members well into their 60s and 70s. CrossFit is about functional fitness — the movements we do help you in everyday life: picking things up, climbing stairs, playing with grandkids.

What if I can't do the workout?

You CAN do the workout — just YOUR version of it. Every movement has a scaled option. Can't do a push-up? Do it on your knees. Can't run? Bike. Can't lift the prescribed weight? Use lighter weight or even PVC pipe. We scale everything.

We have options for every budget: $20 drop-in, class packs starting at $45, and unlimited memberships. After your 3 free classes, a coach will help you find the right fit. No pressure — we want this to work for YOU.

What makes CrossFit Blaze different from other gyms?

Three things: (1) Owner-coached since 2009 — we're not a franchise, we're a family. (2) Small class sizes — you get real attention, not just a number. (3) True community — members become friends, and everyone celebrates your wins.
